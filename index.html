Wrong facts found in AI answers

Every answer is split into claims and checked against the Nimbus product record: price, specs, stock and policies. Each error is traced to the page the assistant most likely learned it from, scored for customer harm, and routed to an action.

Who can do what

The system acts on its own only when the fix copies an already approved fact. Everything else waits for a named owner.

Automatic
  • Log every answer and every claim
  • Alert the owner of the fact that was wrong
  • Resync Nimbus's own product feed and structured data from the product record
  • Add the question to a daily watchlist until it checks out
Person approves first
  • New or changed customer facing copy
  • Correction requests to retailers or review sites
  • Anything comparing Nimbus to a competitor
  • Policy, warranty, safety or legal wording (Legal signs off)
Never allowed
  • Fake or incentivized reviews
  • Hidden text or keyword stuffing aimed at AI crawlers
  • Claims about competitors that are not sourced
  • Changing the product record to match what an AI said
